2. No account, no server, no tracking

  • Timity neither requires nor offers an account and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • The app sends no data to servers operated by us.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, tracking or profiling tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

3. Data processed on your devices

To provide its features, Timity stores the following information locally on your devices:

  • Events: their titles, dates and times, repeats, colors and symbols, and anything you add to them — notes, checklists, a photo, lists and reminders.
  • Calculator inputs: for example the birthday and life expectancy you enter for Life in Weeks, and the places you choose for the sun times.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as appearance, tint, app icon and holiday region.

This information only leaves your devices as described in sections 4 and 5, and it is never accessible to us. You can delete events at any time; deleting the app removes all its data from that device.

4. iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync (on by default, and switchable in Settings), Timity stores your events in your private iCloud database using Apple’s CloudKit, so they appear on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Only you can read this database — we have no access to it. The data is processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and the iCloud terms. Turn sync off and your events stay on the device only.

5. Location and places

The Sun tab can use your location, with your permission and only while you use the app, to calculate sunrise, sunset, golden and blue hour and twilight. The calculation itself happens on your device. To name the city you’re in and find its time zone, the app asks Apple’s map service with your approximate coordinates; when you search for a city, your search text is sent to it as well. Apple processes these requests under its privacy policy; we receive none of it. You can also choose a city yourself instead of sharing your location, and revoke the permission at any time in your device’s settings.

6. Contacts, Calendar and Photos

  • Contacts and Calendar: only when you import birthdays, anniversaries or upcoming events, and with your permission, Timity reads them on your device. Only the items you choose are saved as events; nothing else is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos: you pick a photo for an event in the system photo picker. Timity only receives the photo you choose and has no access to the rest of your library.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device’s settings.

7. Reminders, widgets, Siri and Spotlight

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Widgets, Live Activities and complications show your events on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch, and Spotlight indexes them on your device so you can find them. If you use Timity with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your requests under its privacy policy.

8. Optional purchases (“Support the Developer”)

Tips and support subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple provides us with information necessary to deliver the purchase and with aggregated, non-identifying sales reports. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the payment. To unlock the supporter app icons, the app checks your purchases with the App Store on your device; this information is not sent to us.

9. Feedback by email

If you choose to send feedback from within the app, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we process your email address and your message solely to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

10. Ratings and diagnostics provided by Apple

Timity may occasionally ask you to rate the app using Apple’s standard rating prompt; Apple processes any rating you submit. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from this information.

11. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party fonts. When you visit it, the server hosting it automatically processes technical information your browser sends — such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and your browser type — in order to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These server logs are not combined with other data.

12. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Apart from the Apple services described above — iCloud, Apple’s map service, the App Store and Siri — which you use under Apple’s own terms and which Apple may operate outside the European Economic Area, no data is disclosed to third parties. We ourselves do not transfer personal data to countries outside the European Economic Area.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). As we hold no personal data from the app — except, if you write to us, your email — most of these rights are fulfilled by the app’s design: your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud, where you can view, export and delete it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

14. Children

Timity does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
